You hear a tenth of a second of a song and try to name it. If you can't, you can hear more. Five chances, and using fewer scores better.

How to play

  • Tap the big circular button to hear the clip.
  • Type into Name that track and pick from the suggestions.
  • Stuck? Tap Skip to hear a longer clip. The stages are 0.1s, 0.5s, 2s, 8s and 15s.
  • Get it right and the next round moves up a difficulty level.

Changing what you play

The menu button at the top left opens everything else: difficulty from Easy to Impossible, era from the 80s to the 2020s, 22 genres, and the display options including the stage light.

Playing one artist

Type any artist into the artist search in that menu and the game plays their catalogue instead of the general pool. Difficulty then means how well known the track is for that artist, so Easy is their signature song and Impossible is the one nobody streams.

Common questions

The clip won't play. The audio is streamed from Apple's music catalogue, so the app needs a connection. If a track won't load the app moves on to another one.

Do I need Apple Music? No. The app plays the public 30 second previews. No subscription, no account, no sign-up.

A song is missing or wrong. Track details come from Apple's catalogue. Email below and it can be looked at.

My settings reset. Volume and display options are remembered. Difficulty, era, genre and artist reset when you close the app fully, so every fresh open starts a new game.
