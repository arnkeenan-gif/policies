Privacy Policy — last updated 5 September 2026

Songspot does not collect, store or share any personal information about you.

What stays on your device

The app remembers your volume and display options, such as the stage light and whether cover art is shown, so the app looks the way you left it. Your difficulty, era, genre and chosen artist are not saved: closing the app fully starts a fresh game. All of this is stored on your device using the operating system's own settings storage. It is never uploaded, never sent to us, and it is deleted when you delete the app.

What we do not do

  • No account, sign-up or login.
  • No analytics or usage tracking of any kind.
  • No advertising, and no advertising identifiers.
  • No tracking across other apps or websites.
  • No data sold or shared with anyone.

Music

Song previews, artwork and track information come from Apple. When you play a clip, your device requests it from Apple's servers directly, which means Apple receives that request in the same way it would for any app or website playing a preview. Apple's handling of that is covered by Apple's Privacy Policy.

If you connect Apple Music for full-track playback, playback happens through Apple Music on your device. Songspot never sees your Apple Music account, your library or your listening history.

Children

Songspot is suitable for all ages and collects nothing from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes

If a future version of the app adds anything that collects data — advertising, for example — this page will be updated before that version is released, and the date above will change.
